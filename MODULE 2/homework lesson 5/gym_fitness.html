My Fitness Routine Card

This page presents a structured weekly fitness routine designed to enhance physical strength, flexibility, endurance, and overall well-being.

Dynamic Warm-Up

Begin the day with gentle mobility exercises and dynamic stretches to prepare your muscles and joints for physical activity.

Time: 10 minutes

Cardiovascular Training

Perform brisk jogging, jumping rope, or high-knee exercises to elevate your heart rate and improve cardiovascular endurance.

Time: 25 minutes

Full-Body Strength

Complete squats, lunges, push-ups, and planks to develop muscular strength, stability, and functional fitness.

Time: 20 minutes

Flexibility and Mobility

Practise controlled yoga poses and mobility exercises to enhance flexibility, balance, posture, and range of motion.

Time: 20 minutes

Evening Walk

Take a refreshing walk at a comfortable pace to remain active, reduce stress, and promote mental relaxation.

Time: 30 minutes

Recovery and Hydration

Allow your body to recover through adequate rest, proper hydration, nutritious meals, and sufficient sleep.

Time: Throughout the day

Fitness Tip: Maintain consistency, listen to your body, and combine regular exercise with balanced nutrition and adequate recovery for sustainable fitness.
